# Trades industry pain points (UK focus)

Status: draft · Last updated: 2026-10-01

**Provenance:** compiled from web searches during early research (forums, news, vendor blogs). Source URLs for the first-pass findings were not retained in this export; re-capture before relying on any figure. Items marked (V) come from vendors with an interest in the claim; items marked (A) are single anecdotes.

## Owner-side complaints
- **Lead marketplaces (loudest complaint).**
  - Shared leads: reported ~3 traders per enquiry on Rated People, ~5 on Bark, 10+ on Checkatrade (V). Reported conversion around 1 job per 5–8 paid leads (V).
  - Checkatrade: forum reports of £300–£500/month on 12-month contracts despite advertised plans from £30 (A).
  - MyBuilder: fees reported as non-refundable even when the homeowner stops responding (A).
- **Cash flow:** payment taking 30–90 days while materials and payroll are immediate (V).
- **Staffing and owner as bottleneck:** replacing an experienced employee reported as costly (V); owners stretched across quoting, site work and admin.
- **Overlapping trades/unqualified competition** (A, US forum; check UK equivalent).
- **Insurance cost** (A, US forum; check UK).
- **Job satisfaction:** a Reddit-comment analysis of UK workers ranked electricians among the least happy, citing physical demands and long weeks (single analysis).
- **Sales spam:** owners report many calls selling CRMs, lead generation and SEO (A).

## Customer-side complaints
- **Ghosting/non-response:** one homeowner contacted 25 contractors for a leak; 6 showed up, 3 followed up (A). Small jobs fare worst; large-ticket jobs get callbacks.
- **Deposit-and-disappear:** deposits taken, work never starts (BBB case, A).
- **Basics of service:** an older (2012) Consumer Reports survey found no-shows top, then rushing, not listening, and mess; cost overruns ranked lowest. Dated.

## Patterns relevant to this product
- Both sides share one grievance: missed communication. Owners lose money on missed calls; customers feel ghosted.
- Complaints about ghosting after site visits are a different problem (outbound quote-chasing), not solved by a missed-call service. Roadmap: "Later".

## Caveats
Anecdotes from single threads are not representative. Many statistics are vendor-sourced. US sources should not be generalised to the UK without checking.
